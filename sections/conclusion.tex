\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This study examines how history encoding, future representation, and world--action
information flow affect dynamic manipulation under matched observation, training,
and execution conditions. Its outcome will be a condition-specific design choice,
\placeholder{supported Q1--Q3 findings and selected instance}; numerical results
and external validation remain pending. The current scope is short-horizon,
single-arm manipulation of task-specified rigid objects. It does not establish
language-based target selection, long-horizon planning, deformable-object control,
or unseen action-conditioned contact dynamics. Unannounced events require new
observations, and forecast confidence alone provides no safety guarantee. The
final claims must retain any negative results and interactions that alter the
design choice, rather than presuppose that each study axis improves performance.
