\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduce \ours, a native 4D world--action model that addresses a tension at the
core of dynamic manipulation: predicting a moving scene takes time, and that time lets
the scene move further. By allocating precision by role, with a metric SE(3) trajectory
for the target, sparse 3D flow for its surroundings, and the current frame for
everything static, \ours is precise where contact is decided and acts in 15.1\,ms.
Together with \bench, which evaluates every policy at its native latency, it offers a
model and a benchmark for building and evaluating policies that act on time.
