\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \ours, a native 4D world--action model that allocates prediction precision
by role, with precise prediction for the target, coarse prediction for its surroundings,
and none for the static scene. This allocation alleviates the tension between accurate
prediction and timely action. \ours acts in 15.1\,ms and outperforms the strongest
baselines on \bench, DOMINO, and real robots. We also introduced \bench, which evaluates
every policy at its native latency and shows that paused evaluation overestimates slow
policies. \ours currently assumes a rigid target whose pose can be tracked online, and
we leave articulated and deformable targets to future work.
