\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\ours spends precision where contact depends on it: it models the target's metric
motion precisely and the whole scene coarsely, and uses these forecasts only to
train the representation from which it acts. It improves on reactive, dynamic, and
world--action policies on \bench, on DOMINO, and on six real-robot tasks.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\ours requires a specified rigid target and reliable estimates of its pose, and its
forecast does not model how the robot's own contact changes the target's motion. Its
target-trajectory head predicts a single future per horizon and does not represent
uncertainty or multimodal motion, which an uncertainty-aware forecast could address.
