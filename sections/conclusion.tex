\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Structured object-motion history benefits the Q1 reference, and a frozen-policy
intervention confirms dependence on its correctness. Visual-history readouts
and action sensitivity alone do not establish control utility.
Future supervision has conditional value: sparse trajectories give modest
point-estimate gains, while dense 2-D displacement is adverse. With
CNN/reconstruction, the trajectory condition exceeds both seed-0 controls;
its flow/pose advantage concentrates on phase passage. Its score falls from
66.79\% to 61.21\% at seed 1; matched-control repeats remain pending.

The main screens cover fourteen simulated tasks with privileged target
information, synchronous execution, and one training seed. Granularity,
online foresight, design stability, and robot transfer require further tests.
