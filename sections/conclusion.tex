\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Future supervision has conditional value: sparse trajectories give modest
point-estimate gains, dense 2-D displacement is adverse under the tested recipe,
and difficult tasks retain shared failures. With CNN/reconstruction, the selected
trajectory condition exceeds both matched controls at seed 0; its flow/pose
advantage is concentrated on phase passage. Its score falls from 66.79\% to
61.21\% at seed 1, with matched-control repeats pending.

The main screens cover fourteen simulated tasks with privileged target
information, synchronous execution, and one training seed. Granularity,
online foresight, design stability, and robot transfer require further tests.
