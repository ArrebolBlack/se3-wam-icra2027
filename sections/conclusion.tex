\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Adding target and scene prediction objectives does not automatically improve
a motion-informed policy: sparse trajectories give modest point-estimate gains,
dense 2-D displacement is adverse under the tested recipe, and difficult tasks
retain shared failures. Under the CNN/reconstruction recipe, the selected
trajectory condition exceeds both matched controls; its advantage over
flow/pose is concentrated on phase passage. Together with the history results,
this makes the input and training
recipe essential to interpreting representation effects.

The main screens cover fourteen simulated tasks with privileged target
information, synchronous execution, and one training seed. Granularity,
online foresight, design stability, and robot transfer require further tests.
