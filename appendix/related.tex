\section{Additional Related Work}
\label{app:related}
This appendix extends Sec.~\mainref{sec:related_work} with work that the main text
covers only briefly.

\paragraph{Dynamic manipulation and latency.}
Classical systems compensate latency with predictive tracking \cite{azuma1994} and
visual servoing \cite{hutchinson1996}, and catch or intercept moving objects by
planning on estimated trajectories \cite{hove1991,allen1993,baeuml2010}, later
combined with grasp and reachability planning
\cite{marturi2019dynamic,akinola2021dynamic}. Learned visuomotor policies
\cite{act,diffusionpolicy} and vision--language--action models
\cite{rt1,rt2,octo,openvla,pi0,pi05} generalize across objects and instructions,
and most of them are trained and evaluated on scenes that stay still while the
model computes. Among learned methods for moving scenes, DynamicManip synthesizes
dynamic demonstrations from a single static one \cite{dynamicmanip}, and DynaWM
refines the action chunk of a base VLA for moving objects \cite{dynawm}.

\paragraph{World models and world--action models.}
World models have been used for planning with visual foresight
\cite{finn2017deep}, for reinforcement learning in a learned latent space
\cite{dreamerv3,tdmpc2}, and for planning on pretrained visual features
\cite{dinowm,vjepa2}. In manipulation, generated frames or subgoal images have been
converted into actions \cite{unipi,susie,cotvla}. Studies of WAMs compare how the
video and action streams should exchange information
\cite{openwam_alpha,openwam} and how long and how compressed their context should
be \cite{longwam,pactwam}. Further forecast targets include motion-weighted video
\cite{dcwam} and optical flow or motion vectors \cite{flowwam,hifvla}.

\paragraph{3D and motion representations.}
Object-centric point clouds improve generalization \cite{groot}, and 3D and 4D VLAs
bring 3D and spatiotemporal inputs to large models \cite{3dvla,4dvla}. Dense pixel
motion has also been predicted as an intermediate representation
\cite{langtomo,dawn,fofpred}. Beyond SE(3) decompositions, 3D dynamic scene
memories \cite{dsr} and scene flow estimation \cite{flownet3d,raft3d} model how
scenes move. Point trackers, pose estimators, and feed-forward reconstruction models
\cite{cotracker,spatialtracker,foundationpose,vggt,d4rt} extract such motion
labels from video; our DROID pretraining uses the 3D point tracks that PointWorld
extracted \cite{pointworld} (Appendix~\ref{app:pretraining}).
