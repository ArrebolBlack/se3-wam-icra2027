This supplement is organized as follows.
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}
\item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{app:implementation}} (Sec.~\mainref{sec:method}) derives the error
decomposition behind the allocation of precision and details the inputs, the forecast
heads and losses, the action stream, and training on \bench and DROID.
\item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{app:benchmark}} (Sec.~\mainref{sec:setup}) defines the tasks and roles of
\bench, the data and splits, the real-time executor and latency measurement, the
baselines and ablation controls, and the statistics.
\item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{app:results}} (Secs.~\mainref{sec:main_results}--\mainref{sec:principles}) reports per-task results,
further protocols and latency, DOMINO, the ablations by training seed, robustness to
pose noise, and forecast diagnostics.
\item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{app:real}} (Sec.~\mainref{sec:real}) describes the real-robot setup, tracking,
and trial protocol, and gives detailed results and failures.
\item \textbf{Appendix~\ref{app:design}} reports the smaller-scale design studies that selected the
encoder, history, forecast targets, and coupling of the final model.
\end{itemize}
The supplementary video shows real-robot trials of \ours and the strongest baseline on
all six tasks, \bench episodes of every task family with the forecasts of the
training-time heads, and failure cases.
