This supplement gives the method and training details of \ours, from the error
decomposition that motivates its design to pretraining on real robot data
(Appendix~\ref{app:implementation}); the benchmark and the evaluation protocol
(Appendix~\ref{app:benchmark}); additional simulation results, including the
per-seed ablations (Appendix~\ref{app:results}); the real-robot experiments
(Appendix~\ref{app:real}); and the smaller-scale design studies that selected the
components of the final model (Appendix~\ref{app:design}). The supplementary video
shows real-robot trials of \ours and the strongest baseline on all six tasks with the
tracked poses overlaid, \bench episodes of every task family with the forecasts of
the training-time heads, and failure cases.
Colored dashes and bracketed entries denote values to be filled from the final runs.
